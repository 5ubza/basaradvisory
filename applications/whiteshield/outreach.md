# Whiteshield — Application Playbook

## Sequence

| # | Action | When |
|---|--------|------|
| 1 | Apply on **whiteshield.com careers site only** (not LinkedIn Easy Apply) | Now |
| 2 | Note in the form you are open to Riyadh / Dubai / Barcelona | With #1 |
| 3 | Connect with unit lead + senior DS in AI Economics | +24–48h |
| 4 | Connect with the recruiter / TA who posted the LinkedIn role | +24–48h |
| 5 | Follow-up message with substance to whoever accepts | On accept |
| 6 | Portfolio piece (see below) sent as a link | Within ~1 week |
| 7 | Single polite nudge if silent | +10–14 days |

Do **not** submit through LinkedIn as well. Duplicate ATS records read as sloppy and occasionally get
auto-deduped into a rejection. One channel: the site.

## The portfolio piece

The single highest-leverage thing against 100+ applicants — and it should attack your actual gap, not
restate your strengths. Options, in order of preference:

1. **A small GCC labour-resilience or competitiveness index.** Pull open indicators (World Bank, ILO,
   GASTAT, IMF), build a transparent composite index across GCC states, publish the methodology and an
   R Shiny or static dashboard. Directly mirrors Whiteshield's core product and plays to your labour
   depth. **Strongest option.**
2. **A satellite-derived economic indicator.** Nighttime lights (VIIRS, free) vs. regional GDP or
   non-oil activity for Saudi regions. Closes the geospatial gap head-on. Higher effort, higher signal.
3. **An LLM policy-document pipeline.** Extract structured labour-policy commitments from a corpus of
   published national strategy documents. Closes the LLM gap and uses documents you already know.

Scope it to a weekend. Two pages of write-up plus a link. It is a signal, not a thesis.

Host it on basaradvisory.com so the link is your own domain.
