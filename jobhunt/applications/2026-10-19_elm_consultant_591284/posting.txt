Consultant - Corporate Performance Management (Performance Management Unit), Elm, Riyadh, SA. Job code 591284. Grade I3. Division: Corporate Development. Date: 18 Oct 2026.

ROLE PURPOSE
To contribute to the management and measurement of Elm's corporate performance by translating strategic objectives into measurable indicators, maintaining Balanced Scorecards (BSC) and KPIs, coordinating performance targets and data validation with business units, and supporting quarterly and annual performance reviews and reporting. The role supports evidence-based decision-making and continuous improvement in accordance with Elm's policies, procedures, and strategic objectives.

KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES
1. Strategic Alignment & Performance Objectives: translate Elm's strategy and strategic objectives into measurable performance objectives and indicators; align departmental performance objectives and indicators with corporate strategic priorities; document measurement approaches and expected outcomes.
2. Balanced Scorecard Development & Maintenance: prepare, update, and maintain Balanced Scorecards (BSC); support the annual scorecard development cycle (draft preparation, reviews, revisions, approvals); coordinate scorecard updates with departments.
3. KPI Definition & Target Setting: support business units in defining KPIs, performance targets, measurement methods, and supporting evidence requirements; coordinate discussions on proposed targets; maintain documentation of approved indicators, targets, and methodologies.
4. Performance Data Collection & Validation: collect performance results and evidence from business units; verify submissions for accuracy, completeness, and consistency; clarify data gaps and follow up with data owners.
5. Performance Measurement & Analysis: analyze KPI results and performance trends against objectives and targets; identify performance variances, emerging gaps, and opportunities for improvement; prepare evidence-based findings and recommendations.
6. Quarterly & Annual Performance Reviews: coordinate and support quarterly and annual corporate performance review cycles; track submissions, validation activities, review milestones, and approvals against timelines; prepare performance review materials.
7. Corporate Performance Reporting: prepare periodic corporate performance reports covering KPI results, target achievement, and key performance observations; contribute to quarterly and annual performance outputs used in management and Board of Directors reporting; present data and findings in clear tables, visuals, and executive-ready summaries.
8. Stakeholder Coordination & Performance Improvement: coordinate with internal stakeholders to obtain performance data, evidence, and approvals; track stakeholder commitments and follow up on actions and deadlines; improve performance management methods and practices.
9. Policies, Processes & Procedures. 10. Information Security.

JOB SPECIFICATIONS
Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Strategic Management, Industrial Engineering, Finance, Management Information Systems, or a related field. Professional certifications in Balanced Scorecard (BSC), KPIs, Performance Management, or Strategic Planning preferred.
4-6 years of relevant experience in corporate performance management, strategic planning, performance measurement, KPI development, or a related field. Experience developing and monitoring Balanced Scorecards, managing KPIs, analyzing performance results, conducting quarterly and annual performance reviews, and preparing corporate performance reports for senior management. Experience in stakeholder coordination, data validation, target setting, and identifying performance improvement opportunities.
