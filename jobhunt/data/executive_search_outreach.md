## D. Realistic expectations

Retained firms (Amrop, Pedersen, Egon Zehnder, Boyden, Stanton Chase, Odgers, Korn Ferry, Heidrick, RRA, Spencer
Stuart) work for the hiring client on C-suite and senior-leadership mandates; a 10-year economist profile is at the
edge of their range, so treat this as database registration, not a job application. The agencies (Robert Walters,
Michael Page, Cooper Fitch, Hays) place senior-specialist and manager roles and are the more likely source of a call.
Follow up once, by phone, 3–4 weeks after sending. Scam warning published by Korn Ferry, RRA, Heidrick and Boyden:
genuine contact only comes from the firm's own domain.
